What this is

A plain list of 1502 Android apps made in India, grouped by what they do. Tap any app to open its official store listing. This page has no ads, no analytics and no login.

1057 of the 1502 entries have a verified package id, so their link goes straight to the app page. The rest link to a store search until a package id is confirmed.
