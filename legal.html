Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 October 2026

Swadeshi Appstore is a free, offline directory of Indian apps. This policy explains, in plain words, what the app stores and what it does not.

What we store

We ask for a name and, optionally, an email, and we store the apps you bookmark. Nothing else. No phone number, no location, no contacts, no files, no device identifiers. If you leave the email blank the app works exactly the same.

Where it is stored

Your name, email and bookmarks stay in this app's private storage on your device. They are never uploaded, and there is no account and no server that stores your data. The app downloads a public list of apps so new listings reach you without an update; that request carries no personal information.

Legal basis and consent (DPDP Act, 2023)

We process your name and optional email only because you choose to type them, which is your consent under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. You can withdraw that consent at any time by signing out, which erases them from this device.

Retention

Your name and email stay on the device until you sign out or uninstall the app. Bookmarks stay until you clear them or uninstall. We keep nothing on any server, so there is nothing for us to retain.

Security

Your details sit in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read, and nothing leaves the device, so there is no transmission to intercept. No app can fully protect data from a device owner with full control of the phone, so please keep your device locked.

Cookies and analytics

The app sets no cookies and runs no analytics, advertising or tracking code. It contains no third-party SDKs.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly store a child's data. If a child has used it, sign out to erase the details.

Your rights and deleting your data

You can see, change or erase your details at any time: Sign out removes your name and email, Clear saved apps removes your bookmarks, and uninstalling removes everything. To ask a question or raise a concern, contact our Grievance Officer below.

Cookie Policy

Last updated 15 October 2026

Short version: this app sets no cookies.

Cookies

We set no first-party or third-party cookies, and we run no advertising or analytics cookies. The app has no tracking pixels and no advertising identifiers.

What we store instead

Instead of cookies we keep a small amount of data in the app's own private storage on your device: your name, an optional email, and your bookmarks. This is used only to run the app on your phone and is never sent anywhere.

Why there are no cookies

Cookies are a browser technology for recognising you across visits. This app is an offline directory with no login and no server, so it has no need for them.

Your control

Sign out clears your name and email. Clear saved apps clears your bookmarks. Uninstalling the app clears both.

Third-party sites

Websites you open from a listing (a store page or a developer's site) set their own cookies under their own policies. We do not control those, so please read their policies.

Security Policy

Last updated 15 October 2026

Swadeshi Appstore is built so that there is very little to attack.

How the app is built

It has no server, no account and no login. It stores your name, an optional email and your bookmarks in the app's own private storage on your device. It runs no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code, so there is no third-party SDK that could leak your data.

Network

All traffic uses HTTPS. Cleartext traffic is switched off and only system certificate authorities are trusted, which makes interception harder.

Updates

Security fixes ship as normal app updates from the store.

What we do not claim

We hold no security certification and make no such claim.
